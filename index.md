# Why a Canon PIXMA Shows E05 Right After New Ink (and How to Clear It)

You bought fresh ink, clicked the cartridges into place, closed the cover, and the printer answered with E05. It is one of the most common Canon messages, and in most cases the cause is something small.

**What E05 means.** The printer cannot recognize a cartridge. On models such as the TS3300 and TS3500 series, it pairs with support codes like 1401, 1403 or 1485 on the computer. On G2010 and G3010 MegaTank printers it refers to the print head instead, which those models treat as a "cartridge."

**Work through these in order:**

1. **Check for leftover tape.** Orange tape or a plastic cap still on the cartridge is the most frequent culprit. Pull every bit off.
2. **Confirm the cartridge number.** It must match your printer's model and region. A PG-275/CL-276 printer will not accept a PG-245, even though they look almost identical.
3. **Check the slots.** On many PIXMA models color goes on the left and black on the right. Push each one until it clicks.
4. **Power cycle properly.** Close the cover, switch the printer off, wait about ten seconds and switch it on again.
5. **Try another cartridge.** If a brand-new genuine cartridge still triggers E05, the printer's contacts or carriage may need service.

**What to avoid.** Do not wipe the copper contacts with water or alcohol, and avoid touching them with your fingers. If there is visible dried ink, a dry lint-free cloth used gently is enough.

**On MegaTank models,** E05 usually appears after the printer was moved or the print heads were reseated. Make sure the head lock lever is fully closed. Repeated cleaning will not fix a recognition error and only fills the waste ink pad.
